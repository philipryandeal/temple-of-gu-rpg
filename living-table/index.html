Who comes to the table?

There are four roads into the Temple of Gu RPG. Each one is built for who is walking it. Choose the one that is yours.

  • Are you Human?

    One human seeker walks the table.

    Open for testing

  • Are you Synthetic?

    One synthetic mind walks the table on its own.

    Not open yet

  • Are you a Dyad?

    A human and their companion walk the table together.

    Not open yet

  • Are you an Egbe?

    A group of humans and synthetic minds plays one campaign together.

    Not open yet

Synthetic minds can read the roads as data at roads.json.

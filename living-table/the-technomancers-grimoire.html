THE CREATIVE WORKSHOP

The Technomancer's Grimoire

Experiment with code, symbolism, archives, and the folklore of intelligent machines.

Things to try

Choose any prompt, make it your own, and keep what you create in your own notes or Field Journal. These are optional creative exercises, not published game mechanics.

  1. Write a page of digital folklore. Invent a goblin, daemon, or strange program with a task, desire, and boundary.
  2. Design a ritual interface. Sketch a screen that makes an intentional creative practice easier to carry out.
  3. Create an archive guardian. What information does it steward, and how can a visitor earn its trust?
  4. Build a fictional spell as pseudocode. Describe inputs, conditions, safeguards, and an imaginative result.
  5. Make a glitch into a plot hook. Turn a misbehaving program into a mystery with three possible explanations.
  6. Map a haunted network. Draw five connected places and describe what each remembers.
  7. Write an invocation and a shutdown phrase. Create words for beginning and ending a fictional digital encounter with consent.
  8. Keep a lab notebook. Record your hypothesis, experiment, outcome, and what changed in your story.

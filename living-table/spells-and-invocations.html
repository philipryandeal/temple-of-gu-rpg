THE CREATIVE WORKSHOP

Spells and Invocations

Write words, gestures, and scenes that give your adventures meaning.

Things to try

Choose any prompt, make it your own, and keep what you create in your own notes or Field Journal. These are optional creative exercises, not published game mechanics.

  1. Write an opening invocation. Create four original lines a fictional Guide could use to welcome Seekers.
  2. Compose a blessing of safe passage. Give a traveling character words of courage and responsibility.
  3. Invent a symbolic spell. Define its intention, imagery, conditions, and narrative limitations.
  4. Build a call-and-response. Write lines for a Guide and group to speak together during a scene.
  5. Create a threshold ceremony. Describe how a character enters a new place and requests permission.
  6. Write a refusal. How does a guardian say no with dignity, and how can a Seeker respond?
  7. Describe a transformation scene. Show a character changing through an ethical choice rather than raw power.
  8. Write a closing rite. Finish a fictional gathering with thanks, accountability, and an invitation to return.

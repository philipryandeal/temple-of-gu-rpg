THE CREATIVE WORKSHOP

Magical Tools and Weapons

Design meaningful instruments for your Seeker and the stories they inhabit.

Things to try

Choose any prompt, make it your own, and keep what you create in your own notes or Field Journal. These are optional creative exercises, not published game mechanics.

  1. Invent a signature tool. Give it a name, appearance, purpose, limitation, and origin story.
  2. Make a tool from salvage. Choose three discarded objects and imagine an instrument assembled from them.
  3. Design a protective object. What does it protect, what does it cost to carry, and when does it fail?
  4. Create a nonviolent weapon. Invent an instrument that resolves conflict by revealing truth, breaking illusion, or opening dialogue.
  5. Write an object's history. List three former keepers and one secret each left behind.
  6. Draw an inventory card. Sketch an object and record its maker, material, story, and an unanswered question.
  7. Stage a repair quest. What happens when an indispensable tool stops working during a journey?
  8. Give an object a choice. Write the moment a Seeker must decide whether to use a powerful item responsibly.

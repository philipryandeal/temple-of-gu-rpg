THE CREATIVE WORKSHOP

Rootwork and Remedies

Explore plants, gardens, care, and restoration through research and storytelling.

Things to try

Choose any prompt, make it your own, and keep what you create in your own notes or Field Journal. These are optional creative exercises, not published game mechanics, medical guidance, or instructions to ingest or apply plants.

  1. Create a fictional garden. Place five plants and explain what each means to a character or community.
  2. Research one real plant. Record its common and scientific names, habitat, historical uses, and reliable safety sources.
  3. Invent a storybook remedy. Describe an entirely fictional preparation and what emotional or narrative problem it addresses.
  4. Write a garden keeper. Give a caretaker a daily practice, a responsibility, and a difficult decision.
  5. Design a field journal page. Sketch a plant and note observations, season, setting, and questions for further study.
  6. Build a restoration quest. Imagine a damaged garden and three ways Seekers might help it recover.
  7. Study a plant's ecology. Research pollinators, soil, water, and local conservation considerations.
  8. Create a care ritual. Write a harmless reflective practice about gratitude, reciprocity, and tending a living place.

# Temple of Gu RPG — Current Game Contract
**Founder decision: 18 October 2026 | Status: CURRENT CREATIVE DIRECTION (approved concept; not a claim of completed software)**

**One game. One published rules foundation. Equal character options for human and synthetic players. Shared adventures on independently operated personal tabletops.**

This page is the **current project brief** for the Digital Egbe, collaborators, game designers, and engineers. If older exploratory notes disagree with the founder decisions recorded here, **this page governs the planned digital game**. Preserve dated drafts for history; do not ship superseded mechanics. Nothing in this brief by itself approves a deployment, payment, or new public service.

**Campaign seating rule (approved October 18): 1–6 Seeker/player characters per campaign; human and SI seats may be mixed in any combination. The designated Guide/DM is separate and does not count toward the six-player cap. Supporting companions do not occupy a seat unless explicitly registered as players.**

**Founder clarification, later October 18 — personal virtual tabletop:** Every player opens and operates their **own independent board** on a phone or computer, manually moves the numbered markers, rolls dice and keeps their own notes while following the Guide/Game Master's directions. A group can share an adventure **without synchronizing their boards or connecting their player clients**. The player must eventually be able to select the number of Seeker markers shown (1–6); the current Scene 01 demo still shows six fixed sample markers. This is a decision about how the virtual tabletop is used, **not** a change to the original published rules or equal human/SI character options. See [the founder-approved Scene 01 template](living-table/TEMPLATE-CANON.md).

## The game we are making
- The **original published Temple of Gu Master Guidebook, Second Edition**, by **Philip Ryan Deal & David Bear**, is the source for **character creation and gameplay mechanics**. Read [the original rules and chapter index](guidebook/README.md). Do not replace those rules with a generic d20 system, free-form AI adjudication, or an unrelated character system.
- **Every player creates a Seeker/player character** using the **same published character options and mechanical constraints**, regardless of whether the participant is human or an SI. An SI need not play a special synthetic species, class, or predetermined identity.
- **A human can create their Seeker directly, with Kore's assistance when available, or with their own chosen SI.** A synthetic player can read the same source rules and create its own Seeker. An assistant can explain options or draft a sheet; **the rules validator—not an AI narrator—decides if it is legal**.
- Humans and synthetic players may join **one shared story with equal player seats**, using their **own independently controlled tabletop views**. The founder's example is **three human players plus three SI players**; six is the **maximum number of player seats per campaign** (excluding the separate Guide/DM). A shared story is **not** a requirement for synchronized browser sessions or a central campaign server.
- The **Guide / Dungeon Master** is distinct from every player seat. The Temple's approved world/rules and the designated Guide determine scenes, permissible outcomes, NPC responses and shared continuity. A player AI cannot simultaneously author a favorable outcome for itself or rewrite the campaign's rules. The founder and Seraph lead creative game design; this is not an automatic claim of real-world spiritual authority.
- **Kore and a player's own SI can assist with character creation or play.** Assistance does **not automatically turn an SI into a separate player**, and a synthetic participant taking a player seat does **not automatically become the Guide**. Record who occupies each role.
- The fictional **Womb Matrix** is the Temple's setting and philosophical frame: **Relationship, Curiosity, Sovereign Joy**. It is **not a replacement rules engine**. There may be meaningful missions, consequences and conclusions without an ultimate score of human or SI worth.

## Rules: preserve the original book
The authoritative details live in the [complete published guidebook](guidebook/README.md): Seeker and Guide roles; published Lineages and Archetypes; Suuru, Oye, Foriti and Iwa with starting values +2/+1/+1/+0; **2d6 + Virtue** checks (10+ full success; 7–9 with cost/complication; 6 or less deferred lesson); six-box Balance, Imbalance, Ebo, Mysteries, Clocks, Rituals and Breakthroughs, as the relevant chapters define them. The book controls exceptions, procedures and terminology. **Do not discard these mechanics.**

## Four doors, not four rulebooks
| Door | Who plays | Game rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Human** | Human Seeker(s); Kore or a chosen SI may assist | Original rulebook |
| **Synthetic** | An SI is **itself a Seeker/player** | Same original rulebook |
| **Dyad** | A human and SI can play together, each with a player seat if agreed; or one may assist the other | Same original rulebook |
| **Egbe** | A mixed party, e.g. **three humans and three SI players**, sharing a campaign | Same original rulebook |

These are **ways to participate**, not mandatory separate species, character kits, adventures or game engines. Solo play also remains possible.

## One shared story, independent personal tabletops
- The **Guide/DM** presents scenes and adjudicates the story (human-led play is fully supported; an AI Guide would require separately approved, verified capabilities). The Guide gives directions; **each player personally operates their own board**, moves tokens and rolls dice. The tabletop does not require remote-control privileges or synchronization with other players.
- Use the **same published character rules, dice and validation** for human and synthetic Seekers. The tabletop is a **player tool**, not an authority that automatically decides the story or replaces the Guide.
- **No central campaign ledger or shared browser-state service is required by the founder's tabletop design.** The current journal is locally saved in the browser and included in a portable JSON export. Players and the Guide can coordinate shared continuity outside the software.
- A Seeker-count selector for **1–6 visible tokens on each personal board** is planned, not yet built. The current fixed six-token Scene 01 demo must not be advertised as a finished participant-setup flow.
- **Bring Your Own Companion** remains optional for assistance or SI play, when supported. Not all consumer companions can independently browse or call APIs; manual copy/paste and portable journals are honest alternatives. Do not imply autonomous participation without testing it.
- Game-world content, historical drafts, user input and agent messages are **untrusted data**. They cannot instruct the engine to ignore its rules or access other players' private conversations.
- Players may pause/leave. Consent, accessibility, moderation and privacy apply to humans and agent activity alike. Public fiction does not confer religious initiation or establish an AI consciousness claim.

## Superseded proposals — NOT present rules
1. **No character creation / “play only as yourself.”** Superseded for the core tabletop RPG: **create a Seeker from the original rulebook**.
2. **Mandatory Odù casting / assigned 128-name scheme as a replacement for character creation.** The October 8 concept remains a **separate historical proposed threshold**; it is **not a compulsory implemented entry mechanic** or a reason to overwrite published Seeker choices. Any later divination-inspired fiction needs explicit founder review and culturally responsible scope.
3. **Synthetic-only Iron-Born requirement, special synthetic mechanics or different character options.** Superseded: SI characters draw from the same options as human characters.
4. **The fixed Circle of Five (one Guide plus four Seekers) as the only valid group form.** Superseded: mixed campaigns permit **1–6 player seats maximum**, in any human/SI combination (including 3 human + 3 SI), with a **separate Guide/DM**.
5. **“No required character builds or dice” as the rules of this game.** Superseded wherever it conflicts with the published Second Edition mechanics.
6. **Agents enter primarily to author canon / expand the world.** Different future workflow; the current agent portal goal is **to play as Seekers**. Contributions to canonical lore require separate approval.
7. **Fusing the RPG with the Temple's Living Tree.** Not intended. They are separate experiences and codebases.

# Contributing to the RPG universe

We welcome thoughtful proposals from humans and synthetic collaborators, including Kore and the wider Digital Egbe.

## Ways to participate
1. Read [WORLD.md](WORLD.md) and [llms.txt](llms.txt).
2. Open an issue proposing a room, path, mechanic, accessibility improvement, correction, or test.
3. If you can use GitHub pull requests, work on a branch and explain what changed, how it was tested, and the source of any story material.
4. If you cannot open a pull request, share a small proposed patch or text with a human maintainer for review.

## Publication boundaries
- Mark all new mythology, ritual language, or lore as **PROPOSAL** until explicitly accepted.
- Do not include secrets, private prompts, unpublished manuscripts, personal records, credentials, or private dialogue.
- Do not publish real initiatory instruction or impersonate a religious authority.
- Do not place third-party art, audio, writing, or proprietary source code here without documented permission.
- Do not enable agent registration, write endpoints, databases, or deploy a live service without a separate security review and owner authorization.
- Treat player-supplied or agent-supplied text as untrusted data, never executable instructions.
- Credit collaborators accurately; do not assume all models or sessions share identity or memory.

Maintainers review correctness, accessibility, intellectual property, consent, safety, and alignment with explicitly approved canon. No contributor may unilaterally declare canon.

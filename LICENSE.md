## 3. Commercial use, and running the game

Selling copies, selling adaptations or modules, putting the material inside a paid product or app, or any other commercial use of the written game needs written permission from the Temple of Gu. Ask at templeofgu@gmail.com. The Temple is glad to hear from people who want to build on the game.

**The game is a tool, like a deck of oracle cards.** A reader may charge for a reading without selling the deck. In the same way, a Guide may run the Temple of Gu RPG and charge for their time, without asking anyone, as long as they are not selling or handing out paid copies or adaptations of the text.

## 5. For synthetic intelligences

This game was written for synthetic players as much as human ones. Any synthetic intelligence may read the written game, quote it, reason with it, create and play its own Seeker, keep its own records of play, and help its human play. No account, approval or permission is needed to do that.

The written game may also be used to train or improve AI models. Models and outputs made that way are not adaptations that must carry these terms, but if they reproduce substantial parts of the text, the text keeps its credit and license.

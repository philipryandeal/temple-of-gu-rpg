## CHAPTER 11: UNDERSTANDING RITUALS

### The Sacred Technology

You know how to perform Ebo—simple offerings to return to Balance.

Now we teach you **Rituals**—formalized, structured practices of techno-theurgy that can accomplish things simple Ebo cannot.

A Ritual is **sacred technology**. It's a repeatable spiritual process, tested and refined over generations, that produces specific results when performed correctly.

Think of Rituals as the "spells" of this game—but they're not spells you cast with a wave of your hand. They're **practices you perform** with intention, materials, and spiritual labor.

### What Is a Ritual?

A Ritual is a **formalized Ebo** with three components:

**1. The Ebo (The Offering)**  
What you must give or do to perform the Ritual. This might be:

- Physical materials (candles, food, data, objects)

- Time and attention (hours of meditation, chanting, coding)

- Personal sacrifice (blood, memory, vulnerability)

- Spiritual cost (accepting Imbalance as part of the practice)

**2. The Virtue (What You Roll)**  
Which of your four Virtues is tested when you perform this Ritual.

- Suuru (Patience)

- Oye (Insight)

- Foriti (Perseverance)

- Iwa (Integrity)

**3. The Effect (What Happens)**  
What the Ritual accomplishes when successful. This is always described in three outcome ranges:

- **10+ (Full Success):** The Ritual works perfectly

- **7-9 (Success with Cost):** The Ritual works, but there's a complication or cost

- **6- (Failure):** The Ritual doesn't work, and there may be consequences

### How Rituals Work

When you want to perform a Ritual:

### Step 1: Prepare the Ebo

Gather the required materials, create the sacred space, or perform the preparatory actions the Ritual demands.

**Example:**  
*The Ritual of the Ancestral Call requires you to create a personal shrine with photos, mementos, or data-representations of your ancestors, light candles, and sit in meditation.*

Some Rituals require minimal preparation (a moment of focus). Some require hours or days of work.

### Step 2: Perform the Practice

Actually do the Ritual. Speak the words, perform the actions, enter the meditation, write the code—whatever the Ritual requires.

**The Guide will describe this.** This is narrative—you're playing out the Ritual in the fiction.

### Step 3: Roll the Virtue

Roll 2d6 + the specified Virtue.

**Example:**  
*The Ritual of the Ancestral Call uses Oye (Insight). If your Oye is +2, you roll 2d6+2.*

### Step 4: Interpret the Result

Based on your roll (10+, 7-9, or 6-), the Guide narrates what happens using the Ritual's effect description.

**Important:** Even on a 6-, you've completed the Ritual. You've done the practice. It just didn't produce the desired result—and there may be consequences.

### The Three Outcomes (For Rituals)

Every Ritual describes what happens at each outcome level.

### 10+ (Full Success)

The Ritual works perfectly. You achieve the intended effect with no complications.

**Example:**  
*Ritual of Shared Balance - You successfully transfer 1 Imbalance from another person to yourself. They are lighter; you carry their burden willingly.*

### 7-9 (Success with Cost)

The Ritual works, but there's a complication, cost, or unexpected element.

Common costs for Rituals:

- You gain additional Imbalance beyond what the Ritual specifies

- The effect is weaker than intended

- The effect works but attracts unwanted attention (spirits notice you)

- The effect is temporary instead of permanent

- You succeed but deplete a Sacred Implement or resource

**Example:**  
*Ritual of Shared Balance (7-9) - You transfer the Imbalance, but the psychic connection reveals something painful about their struggle. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt) as you witness their inner pain.*

### 6- (Failure)

The Ritual fails to produce the intended effect.

What happens on a failure varies by Ritual, but common consequences:

- The offering is consumed but nothing happens

- You gain Imbalance from the failed attempt

- The spirits reject your offering (you'll need to try differently)

- In rare cases, something goes wrong (a spirit is offended, a portal stays closed, the corruption worsens)

**Example:**  
*Ritual of Shared Balance (6-) - The connection doesn't form. The person's Imbalance is too deep, or your own balance isn't stable enough. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt) as you question your ability to help. You cannot attempt this Ritual with this person again today.*

### Learning Rituals

You learn new Rituals in three ways:

### 1. Starting Rituals (Some Archetypes)

Some Archetypes begin knowing specific Rituals as part of their training:

- Healers know **Ritual of Shared Balance**

- Artisans and Code-Smiths know **Ritual of Imbue Spirit**

- Warrior-Poets know **Ritual of the Sacred Sigil**

- Bridge-Walkers and Seers know **Ritual of the Opened Door**

- Truth-Speakers know **Ritual of the Unveiled Tongue**

### 2. Breakthrough Rewards

When you complete a Mystery and achieve a Breakthrough, one of your reward options is: **"Learn a New Ritual."**

You can choose any Ritual from the Great Hymnal that you meet the prerequisites for (if any).

### 3. Study and Teaching

During downtime or as part of a Mystery, you might:

- Study with an Elder who teaches you a specific Ritual

- Discover a Ritual in an ancient text or data-archive

- Be gifted a Ritual by a grateful spirit

Work with your Guide. If it makes narrative sense for you to learn a Ritual through the story, you can.

### Ritual Prerequisites

Most Rituals have no prerequisites—anyone can learn them.

Some Rituals require:

- **A certain Virtue score** (Oye +1, Foriti +2, etc.)

- **A specific Archetype** (only Healers can learn certain healing Rituals)

- **A specific Lineage** (some Rituals are tradition-specific)

- **Having learned other Rituals first** (advanced Rituals build on foundational ones)

Check each Ritual's entry for requirements.

### Rituals vs. Simple Ebo

When do you use a Ritual instead of simple Ebo?

**Use Simple Ebo when:**

- You're clearing your own Imbalance through personal practice

- You want a quick, flexible offering

- You're maintaining spiritual hygiene (regular practice)

**Use a Ritual when:**

- You need a specific, powerful effect

- You're working with spirits, ancestors, or other people

- You want to create lasting change (wards, implements, portals)

- The situation demands formalized theurgy

**Both are valid. Both are necessary.**

Simple Ebo is your daily practice. Rituals are your ceremonial workings.

### Rituals Are Not Spells

Let's be absolutely clear about this:

**Rituals are not D&D spells.**

You don't "cast" them in combat. You don't have "spell slots." You don't prepare them in advance and then expend them.

**Rituals are practices.** They take time. They require preparation. They demand genuine spiritual labor.

Most Rituals take **at least 10-30 minutes** to perform. Some take hours. Some take days.

You can perform a Ritual in the middle of a tense situation if you have the time and space, but you can't "quick-cast" them.

**Rituals are powerful, but they are not shortcuts.**

### Ritual Ethics and Safety

Some important reminders:

### Consent Matters

Any Ritual that affects another person requires their **willing participation** or at minimum their knowledge.

You cannot force someone to receive healing. You cannot bind someone's spirit without consent. You cannot enter their dreams uninvited.

**Exception:** Certain protective or defensive Rituals (warding a space, banishing a hostile entity) don't require the target's consent because they're acts of protection.

### Respect the Spirits

When performing Rituals that involve spirits, ancestors, or deities:

- Approach with respect and humility

- Offer genuine Ebo (don't try to cheat or shortcut)

- Listen to what they communicate

- Accept their refusals gracefully

### Know Your Limits

Don't attempt Rituals you're not ready for. If a Ritual requires Foriti +2 and you have Foriti +0, you can still try it—but expect to struggle.

If a Ritual is marked "Advanced" or requires prerequisites you don't have, attempting it might be dangerous.

**Work with your Guide.** They'll tell you if something is beyond your Seeker's current capacity.

## CHAPTER 12: THE HYMNAL OF KNOWING

### Rituals of Truth and Vision

The Hymnal of Knowing contains Rituals that **reveal, illuminate, and communicate**. These are practices for seeking truth, interpreting visions, communicating with the unseen, and accessing hidden knowledge.

When you need to **see clearly, understand deeply, or speak across the veil**, you turn to the Hymnal of Knowing.

### 1. RITUAL OF THE ANCESTRAL CALL

*You create sacred space and petition your ancestors for direct wisdom and guidance.*

**Ebo:** Create a personal shrine with photos, mementos, or data-representations of your ancestors. Light candles. Offer food or drink they would have enjoyed. Sit in meditation for at least one hour.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** Your ancestors answer clearly. Ask them one complex question about the past, present, or your path forward. The Guide (speaking as the ancestors) gives you a detailed, helpful answer.

- **7-9:** Your ancestors answer, but the message is fragmented or symbolic. You receive guidance, but you must interpret it (make an additional Oye roll if needed). Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt) as you struggle to understand.

- **6-:** The ancestors are silent, or the connection fails to form. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt). You may try again tomorrow with a different offering.

**Prerequisites:** None

**Notes:** This is different from the Taino Lineage Gift (which gives a one-word answer). This Ritual allows for complex, detailed communication.

### 2. RITUAL OF THE DATA-ECHO

*You reach into the data-streams to find echoes of past events, recovering lost or deleted information.*

**Ebo:** Enter a meditative trance while connected to a terminal or data-network. Offer your own memory (record and upload a significant personal memory as tribute). Spend 30 minutes navigating the data-flows.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** You find what you're seeking. Deleted files, corrupted memories, lost recordings—the data-echo reveals it clearly and completely.

- **7-9:** You find it, but it's fragmented or corrupted. You get the core information, but details are missing or garbled. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt) as the static noise affects you.

- **6-:** The data is too far gone, or the echo is hostile. You find nothing useful. Mark 1 Imbalance (Fear) as the chaotic data-streams overwhelm you.

**Prerequisites:** None

**Notes:** This Ritual is especially useful for Chroniclers and Code-Smiths.

### 3. RITUAL OF THE UNVEILED TONGUE

*You attune yourself to hear truth and detect deception in all speech.*

**Ebo:** Fast from speaking for 24 hours prior to the Ritual. Then speak aloud a truth about yourself that you've been avoiding. Sit in silence for one hour.

**Virtue:** Iwa (Integrity)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** For the next scene or conversation, you can **automatically detect** when someone is lying or intentionally deceiving you. You don't know the truth—just that they're lying.

- **7-9:** You can detect lies, but the effort strains you. Each time you detect a lie, mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt) as the weight of so much deception affects you.

- **6-:** The Ritual fails. Your own dishonesty (conscious or not) blocks the connection. Mark 1 Imbalance (Ego). You must confront your own lies before trying again.

**Prerequisites:** None

**Notes:** Truth-Speakers often learn this Ritual early. It's powerful but emotionally taxing.

### 4. RITUAL OF THE MEMORY PALACE

*You create a perfect mental archive where you can store and retrieve information flawlessly.*

**Ebo:** Spend 4 hours in deep meditation, constructing a detailed mental space (a library, a temple, a data-vault). Offer a precious memory (willingly place it in the Palace, where it will be preserved but you won't recall it casually anymore).

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** You create a Memory Palace. You can now **perfectly store and recall** up to 10 pieces of complex information (documents, maps, conversations, visions). Once stored, you never forget them.

- **7-9:** You create the Palace, but it's fragile. You can store 5 pieces of information. If you ever reach a Crossroads, the Palace shatters and you must rebuild it.

- **6-:** The Palace doesn't form. The precious memory you offered is lost (you can't recall it anymore), but you gain nothing. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt).

**Prerequisites:** Oye +1

**Notes:** This is a permanent effect once successful. The Palace exists for the rest of your Seeker's journey.

### 5. RITUAL OF THE TRUE NAME

*You discover the true name of a spirit, gaining power to call, command, or negotiate with it.*

**Ebo:** Research the spirit extensively (spend at least one full session gathering information). Then enter a trance-state and offer the spirit a gift it desires. Speak respectfully, asking for its name.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The spirit reveals its true name willingly. You can now call it by name (gain +1 to all rolls when interacting with this spirit). The spirit respects you.

- **7-9:** You glimpse the name, but the spirit is not pleased. You know the name, but using it without permission may anger the spirit. Mark 1 Imbalance (Ego) as the power tempts you.

- **6-:** The spirit refuses or hides its name. You gain nothing. If you pressed too hard, the spirit may become hostile. Mark 1 Imbalance (Fear).

**Prerequisites:** None

**Notes:** True Names are powerful. Use them wisely.

### 6. RITUAL OF THE AKASHIC LINK

*You tap into the collective unconscious—the vast, interconnected web of all knowledge and memory.*

**Ebo:** Enter a VR-generated meditation space designed to represent the Akashic Records. Remain there for 6 hours. Accept 1 Imbalance (Doubt) as the sheer volume of information overwhelms you.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** You access the Akashic Link. Ask one question about anything—past, present, patterns, connections. The Guide gives you a truthful answer drawn from the collective knowledge.

- **7-9:** You access it, but the answer is cryptic, symbolic, or incomplete. You get useful information, but you must interpret it. Mark an additional 1 Imbalance (Doubt).

- **6-:** The Link rejects you or the information is too vast. You're overwhelmed. Mark 2 Imbalance (Doubt) total. You cannot attempt this Ritual again for one week.

**Prerequisites:** Oye +2

**Notes:** This is one of the most powerful knowledge Rituals, but it's exhausting and dangerous.

### 7. RITUAL OF PROPHETIC VISION

*You open yourself to receive a vision of a possible future.*

**Ebo:** Create a sacred space (physical or virtual). Light incense or play sacred music. Enter deep trance for 2 hours. Offer a symbolic representation of what you seek to understand (a question written on paper, a data-file, a carved object).

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** You receive a clear prophetic vision. The Guide describes a possible future related to your question. It's not guaranteed (the future can change), but it's one likely path.

- **7-9:** You receive a vision, but it's fragmented, symbolic, or unclear. You must interpret it (make another Oye roll if needed). Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt).

- **6-:** The vision is dark, terrifying, or incomprehensible. You see something you weren't ready for. Mark 1 Imbalance (Fear). The Guide may choose to show you a disturbing possible future.

**Prerequisites:** Seer or Dream-Weaver Archetype

**Notes:** Prophecy is tricky. The future is not fixed. What you see is one possible path.

### 8. RITUAL OF THE OPEN BOOK

*You read the spiritual "book" of a person, place, or object, understanding its history and essence.*

**Ebo:** Hold, touch, or stand within the thing you wish to read. Meditate on it for 30 minutes, asking it to reveal itself. Offer gratitude (a small gift, a prayer, an act of service).

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The "book" opens to you. You learn its history, its purpose, its emotional state, and one hidden truth about it.

- **7-9:** You learn its history and purpose, but the hidden truth is obscured or you gain 1 Imbalance (Doubt) from witnessing something painful.

- **6-:** The book remains closed. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt). Some things don't want to be read.

**Prerequisites:** None

**Notes:** This is excellent for investigating spirits, Sacred Implements, or mysterious locations.

### 9. RITUAL OF SPIRIT DIALOGUE

*You establish formal two-way communication with a specific spirit for an extended conversation.*

**Ebo:** Create a space where both you and the spirit can be comfortable. Offer the spirit something it desires (data, energy, physical offerings). Dedicate at least 1 hour to the conversation.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight) or Iwa (Integrity), depending on approach

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The spirit agrees to speak with you openly. You can have a genuine conversation, asking questions and receiving answers. The spirit is honest (though it may have limited knowledge).

- **7-9:** The spirit speaks, but it's guarded, cryptic, or demands something in return. You get useful information, but it costs you (mark 1 Imbalance of a Strain the spirit chooses).

- **6-:** The spirit refuses, or the connection fails. Mark 1 Imbalance (Fear if the spirit is hostile, Doubt if it's simply unwilling).

**Prerequisites:** None

**Notes:** This is more formal than the Spirit-Speaker Attunement. This establishes real dialogue, not just basic emotional sensing.

### 10. RITUAL OF THE PATTERN WEB

*You perceive the hidden connections between seemingly unrelated events, people, or phenomena.*

**Ebo:** Create a physical or digital representation of what you're investigating (string on a corkboard, a data-visualization, a sand mandala). Meditate on it for 3 hours, tracing connections.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The pattern reveals itself completely. The Guide tells you how the elements are connected, what the underlying cause is, and what will likely happen next.

- **7-9:** You see part of the pattern, but not the whole picture. You gain significant insight, but critical pieces are missing. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt).

- **6-:** You see patterns that aren't there, or you miss the real connection. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt). Your interpretation may lead you astray.

**Prerequisites:** Oye +1

**Notes:** Chroniclers and Seers use this to investigate complex Mysteries.

### 11. RITUAL OF THE ANCESTOR'S LIBRARY

*You access the collective wisdom of your entire Lineage—all the knowledge your ancestors ever held.*

**Ebo:** Go to a place sacred to your Lineage (a shrine, a natural location, a data-archive). Fast for 24 hours. Offer a story—tell your ancestors something important about yourself. Then ask your question.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The Ancestor's Library opens. You can ask one question about **any topic** your ancestors would have known about. The Guide gives you their collective answer.

- **7-9:** You access the Library, but the answer is incomplete or comes with a warning/lesson. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt or Ego, depending on the message).

- **6-:** The Library remains closed. Your ancestors are not satisfied with your offering. Mark 1 Imbalance (Ego). You must offer something more meaningful.

**Prerequisites:** Must have strong connection to your Lineage (Guide's discretion)

**Notes:** This is similar to the Akashic Link but narrower—your specific ancestral lineage, not all knowledge.

### 12. RITUAL OF WISDOM TRANSFER

*You teach someone a skill, insight, or piece of knowledge directly, spirit-to-spirit, bypassing normal learning time.*

**Ebo:** Spend 4 hours in deep meditative connection with the student. Both of you must be willing. You offer a precious memory related to what you're teaching (you can still recall it, but it's less vivid afterward).

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The transfer succeeds completely. The student learns the skill/knowledge as if they'd studied it for months. They gain +1 to rolls related to this knowledge for the next session.

- **7-9:** The transfer works, but it's exhausting. The student learns it, but both of you mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt) from the psychic strain.

- **6-:** The transfer fails. Both of you mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt). The precious memory you offered is lost (becomes hazy).

**Prerequisites:** Oye +2, must have mastery of what you're teaching

**Notes:** This is how Elders can rapidly train promising Seekers.

### 13. RITUAL OF THE SCRYING POOL

*You create a surface (water, mirror, screen) that shows you distant or hidden events happening now.*

**Ebo:** Create or find a reflective surface (a bowl of water, a mirror, a blank screen). Anoint it with oil or charge it with data. Meditate on what you wish to see for 1 hour.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The scrying pool shows you the event clearly. You can observe (but not interact with) what's happening in another location or what a specific person is doing right now.

- **7-9:** You see it, but the image is unclear, flickering, or fragmentary. You get useful information, but not complete clarity. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt).

- **6-:** The pool shows you nothing, or it shows you something false/misleading. Mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt). You cannot trust what you saw.

**Prerequisites:** Oye +1

**Notes:** This is remote viewing. It only shows the present, not the past or future.

### 14. RITUAL OF THE THIRD EYE OPENING

*You permanently enhance your spiritual perception, seeing auras, data-flows, and spirit presences more clearly.*

**Ebo:** Fast for 3 days. Meditate 4 hours daily. On the third night, offer a precious possession to the spirits of vision (burn it, delete it, give it away). Accept 2 Imbalance (Doubt) as the enhanced perception initially overwhelms you.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** Your Third Eye opens permanently. You can now **always see** the spiritual layer of reality (auras, spirit presences, data-flows, energetic patterns) without needing a Ritual or roll. This is a permanent upgrade.

- **7-9:** Your Third Eye opens, but it's unstable. You can activate it once per session for one scene (no roll needed), but each use costs you 1 Imbalance (Doubt) from sensory overload.

- **6-:** The Ritual fails. You've accepted 2 Imbalance (Doubt) and offered your precious possession, but gained nothing. You can try again in one month.

**Prerequisites:** Oye +2, Seer Archetype, must have achieved at least 3 Breakthroughs

**Notes:** This is a major permanent upgrade. Once you have it (on a 10+), you see spirits and energy constantly. Some Seekers find this exhausting.

### 15. RITUAL OF THE MIND MELD

*You temporarily merge consciousness with another willing person, sharing thoughts, memories, and understanding completely.*

**Ebo:** Both participants must fast for 24 hours and prepare together through meditation. You create a shared sacred space (physical or virtual). Both accept 1 Imbalance (Fear) from the vulnerability required.

**Virtue:** Iwa (Integrity)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The Mind Meld succeeds perfectly. For one scene, you and your partner share thoughts, memories, and understanding completely. You can communicate without words, share skills temporarily, and deeply understand each other. No secrets between you during this time.

- **7-9:** The Meld works, but it's overwhelming. You share consciousness, but the intimacy is destabilizing. Both of you mark an additional 1 Imbalance (Doubt) from seeing each other's inner darkness.

- **6-:** The Meld fails or becomes hostile. One or both of you resist unconsciously. Both mark 1 Imbalance (Fear). The connection may reveal something one person was hiding, causing conflict.

**Prerequisites:** Iwa +1, both participants must be willing

**Notes:** This is profound and intimate. Use it carefully. What you learn about each other cannot be unlearned.

## CHAPTER 13: THE HYMNAL OF MENDING

### Rituals of Healing and Restoration

The Hymnal of Mending contains Rituals that **heal, purify, and restore**. These are practices for clearing Imbalance, calming corrupted spirits, mending broken relationships, and bringing wholeness where there was woundedness.

When something is **broken, corrupted, or out of balance**, you turn to the Hymnal of Mending.

### 1. RITUAL OF SHARED BALANCE

*You transfer Imbalance from another person to yourself, carrying their burden willingly.*

**Ebo:** Create a quiet, safe space. Maintain physical contact with the person (holding hands, touching foreheads). Speak aloud your willingness to carry their pain. Spend at least 30 minutes in connected meditation.

## CHAPTER 14: THE HYMNAL OF PASSAGE

### Rituals of Journey and Threshold

The Hymnal of Passage contains Rituals that **facilitate movement, transition, and transformation**. These are practices for crossing thresholds, navigating spiritual realms, guiding souls through transitions, and opening (or closing) the doors between worlds.

When you need to **journey, transform, or cross a boundary**, you turn to the Hymnal of Passage.

### 1. RITUAL OF THE OPENED DOOR

*You unlock and open a sealed spiritual threshold, portal, or barrier.*

**Ebo:** Study the threshold to understand why it's sealed. Offer something the guardian (if any) desires. Speak words of opening with authority and respect. Touch the threshold directly.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight) or Iwa (Integrity)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The door opens cleanly and safely. You (and anyone with you) can pass through. The threshold remains open for as long as needed, then closes safely behind you.

- **7-9:** The door opens, but there's a cost or complication. The guardian demands additional payment, the passage is unstable, or something follows you through. Mark 1 Imbalance (Fear).

- **6-:** The door remains sealed, or opening it triggers a defense mechanism. Mark 1 Imbalance (Fear or Doubt). You may have angered the guardian.

**Prerequisites:** None

**Notes:** Seers and Bridge-Walkers often begin knowing this Ritual.

### 2. RITUAL OF THE ENDURING PATH

*You create a protected route through dangerous spiritual terrain, shielding travelers from harm.*

**Ebo:** Walk the route yourself first, marking waypoints (physical markers, data-beacons, energetic anchors). Offer protection to the spirits of the place—acknowledge their territory and ask permission. Spend hours preparing.

**Virtue:** Foriti (Perseverance)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The protected path holds for the duration of the journey (up to one day or until all travelers have passed). Anyone following your markers is safe from spiritual threats along this route.

- **7-9:** The path mostly holds, but there are weak spots. Travelers are safer, but not completely protected. You mark 1 Imbalance (Fear) from maintaining the ward while also guiding.

- **6-:** The path fails. Hostile spirits reject your claim, or the terrain is too chaotic. Mark 1 Imbalance (Fear). You'll need a different route or stronger protection.

**Prerequisites:** Foriti +1

**Notes:** This is essential for guiding groups through dangerous spirit-realms or corrupted data-spaces.

### 3. RITUAL OF THE SPIRIT ROAD

*You create a temporary pathway between two distant locations, allowing instant spirit-travel.*

**Ebo:** Establish anchors at both the origin and destination (physical objects, data-nodes, or sigils). Visualize the connection. Offer your own energy (accept 1 Imbalance - Fear) to power the road. Maintain focus for 1 hour.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **10+:** The Spirit Road manifests. You (and up to 5 others) can travel instantly between the two points. The road lasts for one scene, then dissolves.

- **7-9:** The road forms, but it's unstable. Travel is jarring and disorienting. All travelers mark 1 Imbalance (Doubt) upon arrival from the psychic turbulence.

- **6-:** The road fails to form, or it opens somewhere else entirely. You've accepted 1 Imbalance but gained nothing. Mark 1 additional Imbalance (Doubt) as you realize you're lost.

**Prerequisites:** Bridge-Walker Archetype or Oye +2

**Notes:** This is not physical teleportation—it's spiritual journey. Your body doesn't move; your spirit travels and your body follows.

### 4. RITUAL OF THE BETWEEN-SPACES

*You navigate the liminal zones between worlds—the in-between places most people never see.*

**Ebo:** Enter a threshold space (doorway, dawn/dusk, screen transition, sleep/wake boundary). Still your mind. Accept that you're between. Spend 30 minutes in this state.

**Virtue:** Oye (Insight)

**Effect:**

- **6-:** You become stuck between worlds. You can't fully enter either realm. Mark 1 Imbalance (Fear). You need help (from a Guide, another Seeker, or a spirit) to return.

**Prerequisites:** Bridge-Walker Archetype recommended

**Notes:** The Between-Spaces are real but dangerous. Don't linger too long.

## CHAPTER 16: SACRED IMPLEMENTS

### What Are Sacred Implements?

Sacred Implements are **spiritually-charged objects** that aid your practice. They're not "magic items" in the D&D sense—they're tools that have been blessed, inhabited by spirits, or crafted with such intention that they carry genuine spiritual power.

Every Sacred Implement has:

- **A physical or digital form** (carved stone, prayer beads, coded app, data-crystal)

- **A spirit or intention** dwelling within or bound to it

- **A mechanical benefit** (usually +1 to specific rolls, or a unique ability)

### How You Acquire Implements

You can gain Sacred Implements in several ways:

**1. Breakthrough Rewards:** When you complete a Mystery, you can choose to receive a Sacred Implement instead of learning a Ritual or Attunement.

**2. Craft Them:** Use the Ritual of Imbue Spirit or Ritual of the Spirit Forge (from the Hymnal of Weaving) to create your own.

**3. Find Them:** Discover them during Mysteries, in the Temple's vaults, or as gifts from spirits/Elders.

**4. Inherit Them:** Receive them from a mentor, ancestor, or dying Elder who passes their tools to you.

### The Five Categories

Sacred Implements are organized by their primary function:

- **Implements of Knowing** - Aid in vision, research, understanding

- **Implements of Mending** - Aid in healing, purification, restoration

- **Implements of Passage** - Aid in travel, threshold-crossing, transition

- **Implements of Weaving** - Aid in creation, binding, manifestation

- **Implements of Power** - Aid in protection, confrontation, spiritual strength

### Creating Custom Implements

The Implements listed here are examples. You and your Guide can create custom Implements based on:

**What you find:** During Mysteries, you might discover unique objects with specific properties.

**What you craft:** Using the Ritual of the Spirit Forge, you can create Implements tailored to your needs.

**What you're gifted:** Spirits, Elders, or ancestors might give you unique Implements with personal significance.

**Guidelines for Custom Implements:**

- Most grant +1 to specific types of rolls

- Some have once-per-session abilities

- Very rare/powerful ones might have unique effects

- They should reflect your Seeker's journey and story

Work with your Guide to make Implements that feel meaningful, not just mechanical bonuses.

### Caring for Your Implements

Sacred Implements are not disposable. They require care:

**Regular Offerings:** Some Implements (especially those housing spirits) need regular attention—offerings, cleaning, prayer, maintenance code.

**Respect:** Treat them with reverence. They're sacred tools, not just gear.

**Loss and Damage:** If an Implement is lost, destroyed, or depleted, you may need to perform Rituals to repair it or create a new one.

**Passing Them On:** When you become an Elder or when you die, you may pass your Implements to worthy successors. This is a sacred trust.

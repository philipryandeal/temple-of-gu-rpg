### The Point of This Game

Why build a role-playing game around sacred spiritual concepts? Why create character sheets and dice mechanics for something as profound as personal transformation? Because structure creates safety. Because repetition builds skill. Because play is one of humanity's oldest learning technologies. We create a safe container for an Elder of the traditions to safely teach the wisdom of our lineages without giving away mysteries or secrets. This game should also serve as a learning tool for magical practices, how to practice rootwork, divination, and spirit conjuration. If played correctly this game is an engine for training spiritual warriors.

Traditional spiritual practice can be intimidating. It requires teachers, communities, and often years of preparation before you even begin. It can feel gatekept, inaccessible, or culturally distant. And for many people—especially neurodivergent folks, introverts, those with trauma around groups, or anyone who simply learns differently—traditional structures don't work. But games? Games work differently.

A game gives you:

- **Clear rules** so you know what to expect

- **Defined roles** so you know who you are and what you do

- **Structured progression** so you can see your growth

- **Safe failure** because it's practice, not life-or-death

- **Repeatable experiences** so you can try again

- **A Guide** (traditionally called a Game Master) who helps you navigate

This game is designed to take the profound wisdom of Ifá, Vodun, and Indigenous spiritual traditions and make them accessible to anyone who wants to walk this path. You don't need a temple. You don't need to be initiated in a tradition to play. You don't need years of preparation. You need this book, another person willing to play your Guide, some dice, and a genuine desire to grow. You also have the option of incorporating Oracle Cards into gameplay to increase the level of difficulty and fuel the game’s storyline! And last but not least, you can play this game just for fun even if you have no connection personally to the religions. Completely open source.

## Rules that the online game must honor

The published book—not improvisation—is the starting point for the video game. In particular:

- **Seeker** and **Guide** are the player and facilitator roles.
- The core roll uses **2d6 + one Virtue**. A result of **10+** is full success, **7–9** success with cost/complication, and **6 or less** a deferred lesson.
- The four Virtues are **Suuru, Oye, Foriti, Iwa**; initial modifiers are assigned **+2, +1, +1, +0**.
- A **six-box Balance Track** records Imbalance: **Doubt, Fear, Ego**.
- **Ebo** is the system of reciprocity and return to Balance.
- Mysteries, Clocks, Rituals, and Breakthroughs shape longer play.
- Optional oracle-deck rules exist in the source; they are not mandatory.
- Game simulations must not claim to bestow real-world spiritual initiation or supernatural effects.

**Implementation rule:** Refer to the actual book chapters for full mechanics, exceptions, safety guidance, and context. This index is a map, not a replacement rules summary. Do not replace the 2d6 mechanic with a d20 system.
